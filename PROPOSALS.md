# Proposals

Changes to how the plugin treats the owner (what it asks, when it stops, what it decides
alone). Learn describes them here with a recommendation; the owner decides.

## The owner sees the heart of the prototype before the makers start

**Decided 2026-11-09, applied in 0.3.0** in this form: three questions up front that words can
answer (voice, products whose feel they like, what to avoid), one best-guess heart screen
handed to the owner the moment it exists, every reaction a recorded ruling, the flows shown
before the walkers run. Amended the same day in 0.3.1: the heart screen comes as two or three
candidate images before the kit is frozen, the owner picks, then one kit; variants of a
whole prototype are never built.

## The owner confirms flows interactively, not from a one-line hand-off

**Decided 2026-11-09, applied in 0.3.7,** by the owner's correction "I prefer more
interactive user flow confirmation": the partner walks the owner through the flows one at
a time, each step with its anchor on the flows page, yes or change per step.

## The stack stage asks what shapes the architecture, and the owner makes the shaping picks

From sumglass, 2026-11-09. The owner, twice: "these important decision, I wanted you to ask
me, or give me more options" and "are we planning to make mobile app or just web app... how
do you know you want to properly architect?" Decided by that correction and applied in
0.4.1: the owner batch and the shaping picks in stack.md steps 1 and 2.

## Every change to confirmed examples needs the owner's yes again, enforced

**Decided 2026-11-10, applied in 0.5.3 and 0.5.5.** From sumglass: after brief 07 was
confirmed, the partner corrected a one-cent sum and ran `reconfirm` without a recorded yes. Now
every change to confirmed examples, however small, needs the owner's yes again, recorded with
`decide.js add NN` before `reconfirm`, which the script refuses without it; the ask goes in the
owner's next stop, not a separate interruption.

## Approval and going live are one question

**Decided 2026-11-10, applied in 0.6.1.** From sumglass: after the owner approved a logo, the
deploy needed a second dialog. When taking a change live is a separate step, the partner asks
once: approve and put it live?

## Walkers go before the owner's yes on a change to a confirmed screen

**Decided 2026-11-10, applied in 0.6.1.** From sumglass: the owner said yes to a changed flow,
then two walkers found gaps that changed what was approved. For a change to a confirmed screen
the walkers run first and the owner sees their findings with the change; a new product keeps
owner first, since a flow they change makes a walk worthless.

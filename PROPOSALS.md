# Proposals

Changes to how the plugin treats the owner (what it asks, when it stops, what it decides
alone). Learn describes them here with a recommendation; the owner decides.

## The owner sees the heart of the prototype before the makers start

**Decided 2026-11-09, applied in 0.3.0** in this form: three questions up front that words can
answer (voice, products whose feel they like, what to avoid), one best-guess heart screen
handed to the owner the moment it exists, every reaction a recorded ruling, the flows shown
before the walkers run. Amended the same day in 0.3.1: the heart screen comes as two or three
candidate images before the kit is frozen, the owner picks, then one kit; variants of a
whole prototype are never built.

From sumglass, 2026-11-09, prototype stage (feedback lines 12, 13, 14, 16). The owner first
saw screens two hours after the reference examples existed, asked twice why they were not
offered a look, had no moment to pick type, colour, density or voice before 81 examples
were built on one inherited look, gave their reactions as corrections rather than as
answers to a question, and wanted to see the flows before the walkers ran.

Today the prototype stage has one owner stop, at the look stage, after the scout and the
walkers. The stage text says "propose a second direction where it matters" but gives it no
moment.

Recommendation, as one reshape of the stage's owner moments:

1. The foundation ends with a stop: the heart screen in two or three kit directions, one
   line on how to open them and what to look at. The owner picks a direction and reacts;
   each reaction is recorded with `decide.js add` and applied before the makers start.
2. During the prototype every owner reaction is a ruling: record it, apply it to the
   examples, note it in `handoff.md`, and invite reactions early rather than wait for them.
3. The Check order becomes: owner look at the flows (one line, how to open, what to look
   at), then walkers, then the review at the look stage. A flow the owner changes makes a
   walk worthless, so the walk comes after.

Cost: one more stop for the owner, early, when changing direction is cheap. It replaces
corrections given late, when they are expensive. Recommended: yes.
